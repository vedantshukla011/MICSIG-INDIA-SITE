# Search visibility plan for Micsig India

## What is in the site draft

The site now has dedicated, crawlable pages for:

- Micsig current probes: ACP1000, CP Series, CP2100 and RCP.
- Micsig Rogowski coil current probes: a dedicated RCP Series selection page and the model-specific product page.
- Micsig oscilloscopes: model-specific pages for MHO1, MHO3, MHO6, MO34, MDO, ETO, TO, STO, AHO, ATO/SATO and VATO, with model tables, prices excluding GST, datasheets and competitive-offer enquiries.
- Micsig high-voltage differential probes: DP Series.
- Micsig optical-fiber isolated probes: SigOFIT Gen 2 and Gen 3.

The pages contain model families, relevant technical details from the supplied Micsig PDFs, product images, datasheet or brochure links, and links to Vianova's India contact details. The home page and catalogue link to the oscilloscope and probe category pages.

## Publishing and Search Console

1. Connect `www.micsig.in` to the Vercel production project as the preferred host. Add `micsig.in` and both `micsig.co.in` variants to the project and permanently redirect them to `https://www.micsig.in`.
2. Keep a unique page title and meta description on each page. Canonical links and sitemap entries now use the preferred `www.micsig.in` host.
3. Verify the `micsig.in` Domain property in Google Search Console with a GoDaddy DNS TXT record, then submit `https://www.micsig.in/sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps report.
4. Confirm the preferred domain and all listed pages load publicly over HTTPS and are not marked `noindex`.
5. Keep VIANOVA INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS' name, Ghaziabad address, phone and email consistent on the site and business listings. If eligible, verify its Google Business Profile and complete the business details.
6. Ask Micsig to link to the India distributor site from its official distributor/contact pages. Also seek relevant links from industry associations, test-equipment partners and technical publications.
7. Use Search Console's Performance report to track India queries and pages. Expand model-specific pages when there is useful, distinct model information to add from the supplied datasheets and keep published prices and availability current.

## Ranking expectations

No developer or SEO tactic can guarantee the first result for every search. Rankings depend on the query, the searcher's location, competing pages, site quality, and signals from other websites. The category pages build relevant coverage for the requested product searches; performance must be assessed in Search Console after deployment and indexing.

Avoid making many near-identical pages that only swap a product or city name. Add useful model comparison, selection guidance and accurate product information instead.
