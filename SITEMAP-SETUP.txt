Micsig India sitemap and Google Search Console setup

Preferred canonical host: https://www.micsig.in
Sitemap URL: https://www.micsig.in/sitemap.xml
Alternate .co.in sitemap URL: https://www.micsig.co.in/sitemap-co-in.xml
Robots URL: https://www.micsig.in/robots.txt

The root sitemap lists the homepage, catalogue, three probe-category pages, and seven product pages. Page-level canonical tags use the preferred .in host. The alternate sitemap lists the same paths on .co.in, for use only if that host serves pages directly.

Google Search Console submission
1. In Search Console, add the Domain property micsig.in.
2. Copy the TXT verification record Google provides. In GoDaddy, open Domain Portfolio > micsig.in > DNS and add that TXT value at the host/name Google specifies. Preserve other TXT and MX records used by email.
3. Return to Search Console and verify the property.
4. Open Sitemaps for the micsig.in property and submit sitemap.xml. Confirm the displayed sitemap URL is https://www.micsig.in/sitemap.xml.
5. If micsig.co.in serves the pages directly, separately add and verify its Domain property with its own DNS TXT record, then submit sitemap-co-in.xml there.
6. After Google processes the sitemap(s), review the Sitemaps and Pages reports for fetch or indexing issues.

If micsig.co.in redirects to https://www.micsig.in, do not submit sitemap-co-in.xml: sitemap URLs should identify the canonical pages rather than redirected aliases. In that case, submit only the .in sitemap. Redirect micsig.in and the non-primary www variants to the preferred host too.

Submitting a sitemap helps Google discover URLs; it does not guarantee that every page will be crawled or indexed.
