Micsig India sitemap and Google Search Console setup

Preferred canonical host: https://www.micsig.in
Sitemap URL: https://www.micsig.in/sitemap.xml
Alternate .co.in sitemap URL: https://www.micsig.co.in/sitemap-co-in.xml
Robots URL: https://www.micsig.in/robots.txt

The .in sitemap lists the homepage, catalogue, oscilloscope landing page, four probe-category pages, and seven product pages. The .co.in sitemap contains the same page paths on the .co.in host. Whenever a crawlable page is added, removed, or moved, update both sitemap files in the same change and keep robots.txt pointing to both. Page-level canonical tags continue to prefer https://www.micsig.in.

Google Search Console submission
1. In Search Console, add the Domain property micsig.in.
2. Copy the TXT verification record Google provides. In GoDaddy, open Domain Portfolio > micsig.in > DNS and add that TXT value at the host/name Google specifies. Preserve other TXT and MX records used by email.
3. Return to Search Console and verify the property.
4. Open Sitemaps for the micsig.in property and submit sitemap.xml. Confirm the displayed sitemap URL is https://www.micsig.in/sitemap.xml.
5. After Google processes it, review the Sitemaps and Pages reports for fetch or indexing issues.

Because micsig.co.in is also owned, add it to the same Vercel project. The .co.in sitemap is maintained in parallel at the user request. If .co.in permanently redirects to .in, submit the canonical .in sitemap to Google Search Console; submit sitemap-co-in.xml only if the .co.in host serves its listed pages directly. Redirect micsig.in and non-primary www variants to https://www.micsig.in to keep one canonical host.

Submitting a sitemap helps Google discover URLs; it does not guarantee that every page will be crawled or indexed.
