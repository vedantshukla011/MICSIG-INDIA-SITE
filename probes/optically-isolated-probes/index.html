Micsig SigOFIT MOIP probes transmit the measurement signal over optical fiber, electrically isolating the probe head from the instrument. The supplied Gen 2 and Gen 3 product PDFs describe model-specific bandwidth, common-mode rejection, noise and attenuator options.
