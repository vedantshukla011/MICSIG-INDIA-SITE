Ask about RCP Series

Rogowski coil probes measure changing AC current using a flexible sensing coil and an integrator. Micsig lists RCP-XS, RCP-S, RCP-M and RCP-L options; exact coil dimensions, sensitivity, frequency range and ratings depend on the selected model.

When to compare a flexible Rogowski current probe

A flexible coil can be useful when the conductor is difficult to reach with a clamp jaw, or when measuring high peak AC currents in power conversion, motor drives, UPS/SMPS equipment and switching tests. Confirm that the measurement is AC and that the selected model’s frequency range and current limit fit the signal.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Micsig RCP a Rogowski coil probe?

Yes. The Micsig RCP Series is described by the manufacturer as a Rogowski AC current probe family with flexible coil options and an integrator.

Can a Rogowski coil measure DC current?

The RCP Series is specified for AC current waveform measurement. For DC or AC/DC measurements, compare the Micsig CP Series or CP2100 models and check the model datasheet.

Which RCP model should I choose?

Compare peak current, low-frequency cutoff, bandwidth, coil circumference, conductor access, sensitivity and isolation rating. Ask VIANOVA with your expected signal and setup so the model documentation can be checked.
