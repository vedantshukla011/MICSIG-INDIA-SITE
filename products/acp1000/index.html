Micsig ACP1000 AC Current Probe

The Micsig ACP1000 is a clamp-style AC current probe for measuring current without disconnecting the conductor. It has selectable 10 A, 100 A and 1000 A ranges, a standard BNC output and compatibility with oscilloscopes.

Key specifications

Range0.1 A to 1000 A
Bandwidth10 Hz to 100 kHz
AccuracyUp to 1%; range dependent
ConductorMaximum diameter 52 mm
OutputBNC; 1.5 m cable
SafetyCAT III 600 V
