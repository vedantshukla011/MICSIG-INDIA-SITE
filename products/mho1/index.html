Micsig MHO1 Oscilloscope

The Micsig MHO1 high-resolution oscilloscope family includes MHO14-100, MHO14-200 and MHO14-200N variants. The family features 12-bit vertical resolution, four analog channels, up to 1 GSa/s sampling and 110 Mpts memory. Verify model-specific bandwidth and functions in the datasheet.
