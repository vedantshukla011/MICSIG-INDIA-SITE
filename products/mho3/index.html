Micsig MHO3 Oscilloscope

The Micsig MHO3 is a 12-bit high-resolution oscilloscope family for electronics, embedded systems and power electronics measurement. The published model options include MHO3-2504, MHO3-3504 and MHO3-5004 with 250 MHz, 350 MHz and 500 MHz bandwidth respectively, four channels, 3 GSa/s sampling and 360 Mpts memory.
