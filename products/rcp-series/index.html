Micsig RCP Rogowski AC Current Probe

The Micsig RCP series combines a Rogowski coil with an integrator for AC current measurement. Coil size, circumference, cable length and electrical ratings vary across RCP-XS, S, M and L configurations.

AccuracyTypically up to 2%
OutputStandard BNC
Coil optionsXS, S, M and L; sizes vary
Coil voltage ratingUp to 10 kVpk on listed configurations

Applications

The brochure lists motor drives, UPS/SMPS power quality, semiconductor double-pulse tests, power converters and AC current monitoring.
